# Cabinet Quote - Oakridge Cabinetry

## Summary

Line-item quote for all base and upper cabinets, $18,400 including installation, valid for 30 days. It is $1,400 over the cabinet budget.

## Key Points

- Glass-front uppers add $1,250; plain fronts would nearly close the gap.
- Lead time is six weeks from deposit.
- A 30% deposit is due at order.

## Relevance

The six-week lead time means the order must go in by early April to keep the June deadline.
